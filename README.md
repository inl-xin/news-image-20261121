Entry for the make.fun bounty `news-image-20261121` (sum up 21 November's
world news in one image), by @inl-xin.

A newspaper-front-page infographic in Japanese anime flat style:
masthead "Saturday 21 November 2026", region bar, the-day-in-numbers strip,
3 hero panels (US stocks plunge into correction — S&P −3.7%, Nasdaq −5.3%,
Dow −950; Bitcoin crashes to $55,130, a 2-year low, then rebounds above
$60,000; Russian missile hits Kryvyi Rih — 2 killed, 60+ wounded),
18 more drawn panels (Saudi intercepts 3 Houthi missiles, Iran downs a US
MQ-9 drone, two tankers struck near Hormuz, 116 clashes on the Ukraine
frontline, Poland fallout, Denmark expels a Russian attaché, Germany–Qatar
€15bn deal, Trump limits contractor info, Trump pushes a Ukraine deal by
January, F1 Las Vegas qualifying — Antonelli fastest, Verstappen on pole,
Nvidia −14% to a 52-week low, crypto liquidations top $2.53bn, Asian
markets tumble 5–6.2%, European stocks close lower, Banda Sea M6.8 quake,
Qinghai M5.6–5.9 quake, Cyclone Xavier weakens to Cat 1, US measles hits
5,838 cases), an Equal Earth world map with numbered dots, "ALSO ON
SATURDAY 21 NOVEMBER" briefs and a red DATE CHECK box.

23 stories · 6 regions · 21 drawn · one page.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `./entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`). The image is a
pre-built asset; `draw.py` is the build-time script that drew it with
PIL and is kept in the repo for transparency.
